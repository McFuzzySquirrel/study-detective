Visual direction study · FND-4

Which case file should Notes Detective open?

Three candidate looks for the cork board, its pins and strings, and the type that frames them. Compare the same evidence scene in each direction, then choose one. This page is a decision aid, not the production theme.

What to judge

  • Cork tone and texture. Does it feel like a working evidence board without competing with the notes?
  • Pins. Each direction shows a domed pin, a flat matte pin, and a flanged tack. Do the finishes read at a glance and stay clear of app chrome?
  • Strings. A solid red association string and a dashed amber provenance string. Judge weight, sag, and whether the two meanings remain distinct without labels.
  • Type. A period heading over system body text. The heading may wear the costume; the reading text must stay neutral and effortless.
  • Control. One 44 × 44 CSS px action. Judge size, contrast, and the focus ring.
  • Density. Imagine 150 cards. Does the direction stay legible, or does it turn into noise?

Open this file directly from disk. It makes no network requests and uses no external fonts, images, or scripts. Record the chosen direction in docs/reviews/style-direction.json.
